Breezone controls your home from your phone, locally, through your own Breezone server, installed at home (NAS, Raspberry Pi, Docker). It talks to your Airzone WebServer and your devices through their local API.

AIR CONDITIONING (AIRZONE)
• Zones: on/off, setpoint, mode, fan speed, sleep; groups per floor
• Schedules of the Airzone app, editable from Breezone
• Temperature, setpoint and humidity history

LIGHTS, SHUTTERS AND VENTILATION
• Lights: on/off, brightness, white temperature and colours, room by room
• Shutters: open and close, per room or per floor
• Ventilation: readings of each room (humidity, CO₂, VOC, airflow) over 24 hours or 7 days

ENERGY AND CHARGING
• Electricity price, and the Tempo colours of today and tomorrow
• Solar: surplus tracking, which can drive the air conditioning
• Charging: follow and control your charger

ROUTINES
• One-tap scenes, with delayed steps ("switch the first floor off in 2 hours")
• A remote control button can start an automation

LINKED ACCOUNTS AND CAMERAS
• Linky, RTE Tempo, Netatmo, Legrand and EZVIZ cameras
• Several sites and servers in the same app, no VPN
• Remote access through your server's HTTPS address
• Users with rights per installation (read-only or control)
• French, English and Spanish

PRIVACY
The app only contacts the Breezone servers you add. No ads, no statistics, no tracking. The password is not kept: the session is stored in the phone's secure keystore.
