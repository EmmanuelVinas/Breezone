Breezone controls your Airzone heating and air conditioning through your own Breezone server, installed at home (Docker, NAS, Raspberry Pi), which uses the local API of your Airzone WebServer.

• Zones: on/off, setpoint, mode, fan speed, sleep; groups per floor
• Routines and timers: "in 2 hours, switch the first floor off", one-tap scenes
• Schedules of the Airzone app, editable from Breezone
• Several sites and servers in the same app
• History and real-time activity feed
• No ads, no trackers; your data stays on your server

The app requires a Breezone server (free, see github.com/EmmanuelVinas/breezone).

Breezone is an independent project, not affiliated with Airzone. "Airzone" is a trademark of its owner, used only to describe compatibility.
